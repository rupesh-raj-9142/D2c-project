Our Heritage & Legacy

From Our Kitchen to Your Table

LAXMI was created to bring the authentic taste of traditional Indian homemade pickles to every home. Inspired by recipes passed down through generations, we combine traditional preparation methods with carefully selected ingredients.

In an era of mass industrial production rushed with chemical vinegars, we believe in patience. Our raw mangoes, Kagzi lemons, and vegetables are washed, sliced by hand, spiced with stone-ground masalas, and left to mature under the warmth of the sun in glazed ceramic martabans.
